WHO THE HELL IS PETER?

MORE THAN A JOB TITLE.

I'm Peter. Designer by trade, problem-solver by nature, and someone who has a difficult time leaving things alone when I know they could be better.

Outside work, you'll probably find me at the gym chasing gains, gaming, experimenting with some ridiculous new idea or building something that may or may not ever see the light of day.

I'm big on personal growth. Not just gym gains (although those definitely count), but learning, improving and becoming a better version of myself.

I'm also a dad. That puts everything into perspective. A huge part of what drives me is building a better future for my daughter: through the example I set, the opportunities I create and the person I choose to become.

I believe in taking responsibility, doing the work and trying to leave things better than I found them. I'm ambitious, curious, occasionally stubborn and probably juggling more projects than any reasonable person should.

The goal isn't just a successful career. It's a life worth being proud of.

BIO // THE SHORT VERSION

BUILDING THE WHOLE THING.

I started as a designer, but I never stayed inside the borders of design. The work expanded into UI/UX, art direction, brand systems, product thinking, monetization, live operations, campaigns, leadership and building things from zero.

I've worked across AAA games, indie games, iGaming, digital products and freelance projects. Some chapters lasted years. Others ended unexpectedly. All of them added something to the next version.
